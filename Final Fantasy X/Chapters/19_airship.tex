\begin{enumerate}
    \item \sd\ during \cs+3 \skippablefmv. Walk down corridor to the next screen, go back in, \sd. Speak to Brother, \sd. Walk towards corridor, \sd
    \item Walk towards camera for 3 screens, \save\, go up the stairs and towards the elevator, \sd
    \winnpsingle
    \item You need 28 Power Spheres and 11 Speed Spheres for the rest of the run
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textit{If you need 5 or fewer Spheres total (Powers + Speeds):}
        \begin{itemize}
            \item Ignore Rin
        \end{itemize}
        \item \textit{If you need at least 6 Spheres total (Powers + Speeds)}
        \begin{itemize}
            \item Buy up to 4 Distillers from Rin (\sd\ before and after talking to him)
            \item The first Distiller can be used on Seymour Natus, it counts as 4 Spheres
            \item The subsequent three Distillers count as 2 Spheres each, they can be used on Guards Fights 1 and 3 and Evrae Altana
            \item If you need more than 10 Spheres total, prioritize Speeds, this is the last opportunity to gain them easily
        \end{itemize}
    \end{itemize}
    \item Take the elevator, go out, \sd
\end{enumerate}
